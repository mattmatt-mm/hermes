Lesson 10 · markets

How an exchange matches orders

  • Price-time priority — The default matching rule: best price wins first, and among equal prices the earliest order in the queue gets filled.
  • The limit order book — Two sorted sides of resting orders; a match happens the moment an incoming order crosses the opposite best price.
  • Order types and how they interact — Market, limit, IOC, FOK and post-only orders each change whether you take liquidity, add it, or get rejected.
  • Auctions vs continuous trading — Opening and closing auctions clear at a single price that maximises volume, unlike the continuous session's incremental matching.
  • Building a toy matching engine — A heap or sorted map per side plus a FIFO queue at each price level is enough to implement correct price-time matching.
