Lesson 13 · markets

Position sizing and drawdown control

  • Risk per trade — Decide the fixed fraction of capital you are willing to lose on one idea, typically well under 2%.
  • Sizing from stop distance — Position size = risk budget divided by the distance from entry to stop, so volatile names get smaller positions.
  • Volatility targeting — Scale exposure inversely to recent volatility so portfolio risk stays roughly constant across regimes.
  • Drawdown mathematics — A 50% loss needs a 100% gain to recover, which is why capping drawdown matters more than maximising upside.
  • Kelly and why to use a fraction of it — Full Kelly maximises growth but is brutally volatile; practitioners typically run a quarter or half Kelly.
