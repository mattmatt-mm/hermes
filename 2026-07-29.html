Lesson 9 · markets

Market microstructure 101: the order book

  • Bids, asks and the spread — The best bid and best ask define the market; the spread is the cost of trading immediately.
  • Limit vs market orders — Limit orders add liquidity and rest in the book; market orders remove liquidity and cross the spread.
  • Depth of market — The stacked quantities at each price level show available liquidity and how large orders move price.
  • Price-time priority — Most exchanges fill orders first by better price, then by earlier arrival — why queue position matters.
  • Liquidity and slippage — Thin books mean big trades walk through levels; execution cost is a first-class concern for quant firms.
