Portfolio management fundamentals

  • Return and risk — Expected return is the mean of outcomes; risk is the dispersion around it, usually measured as standard deviation.
  • Correlation and diversification — Combining assets that do not move together lowers portfolio volatility without giving up expected return.
  • The efficient frontier — For each level of risk there is a best achievable return; Markowitz optimisation traces that boundary.
  • Sharpe ratio and risk-adjusted return — Excess return divided by volatility, the standard way to compare strategies on equal footing.
  • Rebalancing and concentration — Weights drift with performance, so periodic rebalancing keeps risk where you intended it.
